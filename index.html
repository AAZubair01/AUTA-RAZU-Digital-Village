About This Page

Welcome to the AUTA-RAZU Digital Village — the online home for lecture materials, announcements and results for Levels 2 to 5, Department of Radiography and Radiation Sciences, FUHSA Azare.

Each level has its own page containing:

  • Announcements — the latest news for your level, always at the top.
  • Results — published results for tests and assessments.
  • Lectures — the current semester's lecture PDFs, session by session.
  • Past semesters — old lectures stay available in the archive section.

Students: please open the link for your own level only, as shared by your lecturer. Bookmark it so you can revisit your lectures at any time.
